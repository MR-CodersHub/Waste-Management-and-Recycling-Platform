Reference Index

Key Sustainability Terminology

Quick guide to the compliance standards referenced across our publications.

PCR (Post-Consumer Resin)

Plastic material recovered from consumer waste packaging that has been cleaned, sorted, and remelted into new pellets.

MRF (Material Recovery Facility)

A specialized high-throughput plant that sorts commingled recyclables into individual pure commodity bales for end mills.

Scope 3 GHG Emissions

Indirect greenhouse gas emissions occurring across a company's upstream and downstream value chain, including waste disposal.

Cullet

Crushed recycled glass ready to be remelted in furnace kilns, requiring 30% less energy than raw silica mineral batch.
