Zero-Landfill Model

How Our Closed-Loop Lifecycle Works

Every kilogram of waste collected through ReCycleHub is digitally audited, separated by optical sensors, and channeled into closed-loop circular manufacturing.

STAGE 01

Scheduled Zero-Emission Pickup

Customers book via our responsive platform. Electric collection vehicles arrive in verified time windows with calibrated onboard scales.

STAGE 02

AI Optical Sorting & NIR

Materials enter our Material Recovery Facility (MRF) where near-infrared hyperspectral sensors and robotic arms achieve 99.4% sorting purity.

STAGE 03

High-Density Densification

Purified materials are shredded, de-contaminated, washed, and compressed into standard post-consumer resin (PCR) pellets and baled raw commodities.

STAGE 04

Remanufacture & Manifest

Goods re-enter the circular supply chain as new packaging and products. Clients receive auditable ISO 14001 ESG certificates and eco-points.
