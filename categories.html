Contamination Prevention

Top Common Sorting Pitfalls to Avoid

Avoid these common errors that cause MRF equipment jams and landfill diversion failures.

1. "Wish-Cycling" Non-Recyclables

Throwing items in the bin hoping they can be recycled (e.g. garden hoses, bowling balls, ceramic mugs) damages conveyor optical belts.

2. Plastic Bags in Curbside Bins

Thin films wrap around spinning mechanical sorting axles, requiring emergency plant shutdowns. Return them to retail collection points.

3. Wet or Soiled Food Containers

Peanut butter jars and unrinsed milk jugs ruin adjacent dry paper. Always give containers a quick 3-second cold rinse.

4. Loose Lithium-Ion Batteries in Trash

Crushed lithium batteries cause severe truck and facility fires. Always schedule dedicated hazardous or e-waste collection.

5. Pyrex, Mirrors & Light Bulbs with Bottles

Treated glass has a different melting point than container cullet and will create structural defects in recycled glass.

6. Black Plastic Food Trays

Carbon black pigment absorbs near-infrared optical light, blinding optical scanners. Clean and sort into our specialized polymer bin.
