ReCycleHub Platform Inc. (“ReCycleHub”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and the rights and choices available to you when you use our website, dashboards, pickup scheduling tools, notifications, and on-site collection services (collectively, the “Services”).

This Policy applies to residents, enterprise customers, municipal partners, and visitors. It does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that we do not own or control, even if they are linked from our platform.

01 Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Account and contact data: name, email address, phone number, password (stored in hashed form), business name, billing address, and account preferences.
  • Pickup and service data: service address, postal code, access instructions, uploaded photos, material categories, estimated weight, and preferred collection windows.
  • Payment and billing data: payment method tokens, invoices, transaction history, and tax identifiers. Full card numbers are processed by our PCI-DSS-compliant payment providers and are not stored on ReCycleHub servers.
  • Communications and support data: messages sent through contact forms, live chat, email, phone, survey responses, reviews, and complaint records.
  • Enterprise administration data: names, work email addresses, roles, and site assignments of employees that an enterprise administrator adds to a business account.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, referring URLs, pages viewed, features used, and session duration.
  • Approximate location data: derived from your IP address or device settings. We collect precise GPS location only when you enable location permissions for route tracking or pickup verification.
  • Cookie and similar data: as described in Section 04 below.

Information from third parties

  • Municipal and facility partners, including service eligibility, contamination reports, and recycling credit confirmations.
  • Payment processors, fraud prevention providers, mapping services, and analytics providers.
  • Public sources, referral partners, and enterprise administrators who create or manage accounts on your behalf.

02 How We Use Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Provide, schedule, dispatch, and complete collection services.
  • Calculate pricing, rewards, rebates, and sustainability metrics.
  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate users, and manage dashboards.
  • Process payments, invoices, refunds, and credit redemptions.
  • Send operational notifications such as route reminders, delays, and service confirmations.
  • Respond to inquiries, resolve disputes, and provide customer support.
  • Improve routes, sorting accuracy, service quality, and platform performance.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, contamination, misuse, and security incidents.
  • Comply with legal, tax, environmental, and reporting obligations.
  • Send marketing communications where permitted, with an easy opt-out.
  • Produce aggregated or de-identified analytics that cannot reasonably identify you.

03 Legal Bases for Processing

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process personal information only where we have a valid legal basis:

  • Contract: to provide services you request, including scheduling and billing.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure, and improve our services, prevent fraud, and communicate relevant updates, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: for optional marketing, precise location, and certain cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with tax, environmental, safety, and record-keeping laws.
  • Vital interests: to protect someone's health or safety in an emergency.

04 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as pixels and local storage) to operate the platform, remember preferences, measure performance, and support marketing. The categories we use include:

  • Strictly necessary: required for login, security, booking flows, and load balancing.
  • Preferences: remember theme, language, direction, and region.
  • Analytics: help us understand page performance and feature usage in aggregate.
  • Marketing: measure campaigns and, where required, only with your consent.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the platform from working. Where required by law, we display a consent banner and honor Global Privacy Control signals.

05 How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to operate the Services and as described below:

  • Service providers: payment processors, cloud hosting, mapping, communications, analytics, customer support, and logistics partners bound by confidentiality and data protection terms.
  • Enterprise and municipal partners: when your account is managed by an organization or when reporting is required for municipal recycling programs. We share service-level data, not unnecessary personal details.
  • Professional advisors: auditors, insurers, lawyers, and accountants under confidentiality obligations.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of ReCycleHub, our customers, or the public.
  • With your consent: for any other purpose you expressly authorize.

06 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy:

  • Account records: while your account is active and for up to 24 months after closure, unless a longer period is required.
  • Transaction and tax records: at least 7 years to meet accounting and legal requirements.
  • Support and complaint records: up to 36 months after resolution.
  • Route, telematics, and proof-of-service data: up to 12 months, unless needed for an active dispute or legal matter.
  • Marketing preferences: until you opt out, plus a suppression record to honor your choice.
  • Aggregated or de-identified data: may be retained indefinitely because it no longer identifies you.

When retention periods expire, we securely delete or anonymize the information.

07 Data Security

We apply administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS and encryption at rest for sensitive records.
  • Hashed passwords and role-based access controls.
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative and dispatch accounts.
  • Network monitoring, logging, and regular security testing.
  • Vendor due diligence and contractual security requirements.
  • Documented incident response and notification procedures.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

08 Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Receive a portable copy of information you provided to us.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, including direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link or account settings.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

California residents may also request to know, delete, or correct personal information; opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; and limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

To exercise a right, email privacy@recyclehub.org or call 1-800-RECYCLE. We will verify your identity before processing the request. Authorized agents may submit requests with written permission. We respond within 30 days, or within 45 days for certain California requests, and will explain any extension. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision.

Marketing choices: you can unsubscribe from promotional emails using the link in every message, update SMS preferences in your account, or contact us to opt out of postal marketing.

09 International Data Transfers

ReCycleHub is headquartered in the United States. If you access the Services from another country, your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate. Where required, we use Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and additional safeguards to protect transferred information.

10 Children's Privacy

The Services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 16 (or under 13 where local law requires a higher threshold). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact privacy@recyclehub.org and we will promptly delete it.

11 Third-Party Links & Services

The Services may link to third-party websites, widgets, or tools, such as social media pages, maps, and payment portals. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review their policies before sharing personal information.

12 Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control

Some browsers transmit “Do Not Track” signals. Because there is no industry consensus on how to interpret them, we do not currently respond to legacy DNT signals. Where required by law, we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

13 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, or data practices. When we make changes, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will provide prominent notice on the platform or contact you by email where appropriate. Continued use of the Services after the effective date means you acknowledge the updated Policy.
