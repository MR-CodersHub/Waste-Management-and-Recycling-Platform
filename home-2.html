Optical Robotics

Inside Our Next-Gen Recovery Center

Tour the mechanical and artificial intelligence workflows powering 99.4% post-consumer purity.

Tech Layer 01

Near-Infrared (NIR) Hyperspectral

Scans 1,200 polymer items per second, differentiating identical-looking PET, HDPE, and PP plastics down to polymer grade.

Tech Layer 02

Eddy Current Non-Ferrous Ejectors

High-intensity rare-earth magnetic rotors induce eddy currents in aluminum cans, physically launching them into segregation bins.

Tech Layer 03

Ballistic Fiber Separation

Rotary disc screens propel stiff cardboard forward while flexible paper climbs upward and rigid containers roll downward.

Tech Layer 04

In-Vessel Aerobic Digesters

Thermophilic automated organic chambers maintaining 55°C pasteurization, transforming food waste into cured humus in 14 days.

Route Optimization

Zero-Deadhead Electric Fleet Management

Our dispatch algorithms calculate real-time traffic, bin sensor fill percentages, and vehicle gross weight to generate zero-deadhead collection routes.

100% Zero Direct Emission Hauling

Class-7 electric compactor trucks charged overnight by certified wind and rooftop solar installations.

NTEP-Certified Onboard Dynamic Scales

Every container is weighed in mid-air during lift. Real-time mass data writes directly into the customer portal ledger.

Automated Contamination Photo Flagging

Hopper cameras detect non-conforming items during tipping and notify facility coordinators before processing.
