Pickup Prep

3 Steps to Prepare Your Material for Collection

Follow these quick steps to ensure smooth handling and safety for our drivers.

1

Empty & Clean Rinse

Give jars, beverage bottles, and plastic tubs a quick rinse to remove food residue. You do not need to scrape off paper labels.

2

Flatten Boxes & Bundle

Break down corrugated shipping cardboard flat to optimize vehicle bed capacity. Bundle neatly or place in a reusable bin.

3

Place at Curbside or Porch

Set containers out by 8:30 AM for morning slots or 12:30 PM for afternoon slots. Our driver will scan the tag and weigh on site.
