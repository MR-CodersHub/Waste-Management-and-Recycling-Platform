Our Story

From a Neighborhood Pilot to a City-Scale Circular Network

ReCycleHub started with a simple question: what if every neighborhood could turn its waste into a verified, measurable resource instead of a landfill liability?

In 2018, three cargo trikes and a team of volunteer sorters began collecting glass and food scraps across the Mission District. That grassroots pilot grew into an audited circular network connecting residents, businesses, and reprocessing partners across three metro regions.

  • Community-first collection with local green jobs and transparent material tracking.
  • Every load weighed, photographed, and reconciled through our auditable digital ledger.
  • Today we recover more than 40 material streams with a 99.4% optical sorting purity guarantee.
