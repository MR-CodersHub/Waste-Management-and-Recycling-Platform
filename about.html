Our Purpose

Engineering a Planet Without Discarded Waste.

Linear consumption has broken municipal supply chains. ReCycleHub was founded on a simple scientific thesis: waste is simply an unindexed, misplaced raw resource.

By combining on-demand smart logistics with advanced near-infrared spectroscopy sorting and certified chain-of-custody ledgers, we make zero-landfill operations effortless for homeowners and profitable for global enterprises.

Our History

The Journey to Autonomous Circularity

From a pilot neighborhood collection program in 2018 to the region's largest AI-powered circular network.

2018 – FOUNDATION

Neighborhood Micro-Hub

Launched with 3 cargo trikes in the Mission District, diverting 40 tons of residential glass and organic food waste in the first year.

2021 – OPTICAL MRF 01

NIR Hyperspectral Sorting

Commissioned our flagship 50,000 sq.ft. automated sorting center, boosting plastic polymer segregation purity from 82% to 98.7%.

2024 – EV FLEET NETWORK

100% Electric Logistics

Transitioned all urban heavy routes to zero-emission Class-7 electric compaction vehicles, eliminating 840 tons of diesel exhaust annually.

2026 – SMART CITY IOT

Autonomous Sensor Grid

Surpassed 12,000 connected IoT smart commercial bins with automated algorithmic routing and instant auditable ISO manifests.
