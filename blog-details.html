How hyperspectral imaging combined with high-speed pneumatic ejectors achieves 99.4% sorting purity across complex composite post-consumer packaging.

Executive Summary & Key Findings

  • Hyperspectral Leap: Modern optical sorters using 900–1700nm wavelengths differentiate HDPE from PP at 4.2 metres per second.
  • Pneumatic Micro-Ejectors: 128 air nozzles operate with 4-millisecond trigger responses for 99.4% purity.
  • Economic Yield: Purity above 99% unlocks food-grade bottle-to-bottle certification.
